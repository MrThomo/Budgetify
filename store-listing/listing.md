**App name:** Budgetify
**Short description (80 chars):** Budget smarter in English, isiZulu or Sesotho — even offline.

**Full description:**
Budgetify helps you stay on top of your money without needing data all the time.

• Log expenses anywhere — even with no signal. They sync automatically when you reconnect.
• Set monthly budgets per category and see progress at a glance.
• Get an alert when you reach 80% or 100% of a budget.
• Earn points, streaks and badges for good habits.
• Attach a photo of your receipt.
• Available in English, isiZulu and Sesotho.
• Sign in with Google or an email and password.
